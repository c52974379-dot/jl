Privacy Policy

How we handle personal data, including the additional rights available to visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and North America. Last updated 1 February 2026.

1. Who we are

Awakening Culture Technology (Henan) Co., Ltd., Zhengzhou, Henan, China, is the controller of personal data collected through this website and in the course of our business. Our contact details are published on the contact page.

2. What we collect

  • Information you provide: name, company, work email, telephone number, country, and the content of your inquiry or order.
  • Transaction information: shipping addresses, tax registration details, bank and payment references, and correspondence relating to an order.
  • Technical information: IP address, browser type, device type, pages viewed and referring URL, collected through server logs and, where consented, analytics cookies.
  • Clinical or patient information: we do not collect patient health data through this website. Where our training or platform services involve Clinical data, the clinic remains the controller and a separate data processing agreement applies.

3. Why we use it and on what basis

  • To respond to inquiries and prepare quotations — on the basis of steps taken at your request before entering a contract.
  • To perform contracts, including order processing, shipping, invoicing and support — on the basis of contract performance.
  • To improve the website and understand which pages are useful — on the basis of our legitimate interest, or consent where cookies require it.
  • To send regulatory and product updates where you subscribe — on the basis of consent, withdrawable at any time.
  • To meet legal, tax, customs and export-control obligations — on the basis of legal obligation.

4. International transfers

We are established in China and process data there. Where personal data originates in the EEA or the UK, transfers are made under appropriate safeguards, currently the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses together with a transfer impact assessment, or another lawful transfer mechanism. Where you require data to remain in your region for a specific engagement, tell us before the contract is signed and we will agree the arrangement in writing.

5. Sharing

We share personal data only with parties necessary to perform the contract or meet legal obligations: freight forwarders and customs brokers, banks and payment providers, third-party testing and inspection bodies, IT and email service providers, and professional advisers. We do not sell personal data and we do not share it for third-party advertising.

6. Retention

Inquiry correspondence is retained for three years from last contact. Contract, invoice, customs and warranty records are retained for ten years to meet accounting, product-liability and export-control requirements. Newsletter subscriptions are retained until withdrawn. Analytics data is retained for 14 months.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to your personal data, correction of inaccuracies, erasure, restriction of processing, portability, and objection to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing. To exercise a right, email us using the address on the contact page; we respond within 30 days. If you are in the EEA or the UK you may also complain to your national supervisory authority.

8. Cookies

The site uses strictly necessary storage to remember your language and cart selection; these are stored in your browser and are not shared. Analytics cookies, if enabled, are set only after consent and can be refused without loss of functionality.

9. Security

We apply access controls, encryption in transit, least-privilege permissions and regular backup to protect personal data. No transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security; we will notify affected individuals and authorities of a reportable breach as required by law.

10. Children

This website is directed at businesses and health professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be indicated by the “last updated” date at the top of this page.
