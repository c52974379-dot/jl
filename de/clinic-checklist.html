TCM clinic start-up checklist

The list we work through with every partner before we quote a single item — equipment, dispensary, documentation and jurisdiction-specific compliance preparation.

What the checklist covers

1. Establish your regulatory position first

  • Is TCM a regulated health profession in your jurisdiction, and what is the protected title?
  • What is your lawful scope of practice and what must be referred out?
  • Which insurance cover applies to your modalities, and what does the insurer require to see?
  • What hygiene, needling-safety and adverse-event reporting rules apply to you?

2. Treatment rooms

  • One couch, trolley and stool per room, plus a screened changing area
  • Adequate lighting for needling, with no shadow across the treatment surface
  • Lockable storage for needles and sharps containers compliant with local rules
  • Moxibustion ventilation or extraction, and a fire-safety plan

3. Dispensary

  • Granule or whole-herb storage with temperature and humidity monitoring
  • Calibrated scales, mixer and labelling station
  • Goods-in inspection area with a documented sampling procedure
  • Batch segregation and expiry control with a visible rotation system

4. Documentation

  • Consent forms and privacy notices localised to your jurisdiction
  • Patient record system with a defensible retention policy
  • Dispensing records linking prescription, batch and patient
  • Standard operating procedures for receiving, storage and dispensing

5. Supply chain

  • Confirmed test panel and acceptance limits agreed in writing before the first order
  • Batch-level certificates of analysis and origin statements
  • Planned replenishment levels so a cabinet never runs empty mid-week
  • A named contact for clinical and quality escalations
