Key features

  • Evaporation at 45–65 °C under vacuum, preserving heat-labile compounds
  • Brix control loop with automatic feed compensation
  • Two-effect steam economy, roughly 1.8 kg water per kg steam
  • Turbulent falling-film design resists fouling on viscous extracts

Applications

  • Concentrating decoctions ahead of spray drying
  • Syrup and fluid-extract manufacture
  • Solvent stripping after extraction
