Shipping, Returns & Warranty Claims

What to expect from dispatch to delivery, and exactly how to raise a problem so it is resolved quickly. Last updated 1 February 2026.

1. Shipping terms

  • FOB — we deliver to the named Chinese port and load; you arrange and pay freight and insurance.
  • CIF — we arrange and pay freight and marine insurance to the destination port.
  • DDP — we handle freight, import clearance, duties and delivery to your address. We require your full delivery address and tax registration details before confirming.
  • Air freight and courier — used for instruments, samples, herbs in small quantities and spare parts.

2. Export documentation

Every commercial shipment is accompanied by a commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading or air waybill, certificate of origin where required, and — for botanical material — a certificate of analysis, phytosanitary certificate where applicable, and non-GMO or SO₂ statement on request. Certificates of origin for preferential tariff treatment (for example under a free trade agreement) are arranged where the rules of origin for your product allow it; ask before the order is placed.

3. Lead times

Standard instruments and herb supply typically dispatch within 15 to 40 days of order confirmation. Configured equipment and production lines take 45 to 120 days depending on scope. Quoted lead times run from receipt of the deposit and confirmation of the final specification, not from the date of inquiry.

4. Packing

Equipment is packed in seaworthy plywood cases with moisture barriers and shock indicators. Instruments travel in shock-mounted crates with temperature loggers. Botanical material ships in food-grade lined cartons on heat-treated, fumigation-free pallets with container desiccants.

5. Inspection on arrival

Inspect shipments before signing the carrier’s delivery receipt. Note any visible damage on the receipt and photograph it. Notify us within 7 days of delivery for shortage or visible damage, and within 30 days for damage discovered on unpacking. Claims raised later than this are difficult to pursue against the carrier and may be declined.

6. Returns

  • Configured and made-to-order equipment cannot be returned for credit once production has started, because it is built to your specification.
  • Standard catalogue instruments may be returned unused and in original packaging within 30 days of delivery, subject to a restocking fee of 15% and return freight at your cost.
  • Botanical material may be returned only if it fails the specification agreed in writing before the order. Opened food-grade packaging cannot be returned for resale reasons.
  • Custom-manufactured formulations and private-label product are non-returnable.

7. Warranty claims

To raise a warranty claim, email us with the order number, equipment serial number, a description of the fault, photographs or video, and the operating conditions at the time of failure. We will acknowledge within one business day and propose a remedy: remote diagnosis, a replacement part, on-site attendance, or return of the item for repair. Do not return goods without a return authorisation reference; unauthorised returns cannot be cleared through customs.

8. Spare parts

A two-year recommended spare-parts kit is quoted with every equipment order. Consumable items are held in stock for the life of the model, and critical spares dispatch within 5 business days of order. After the warranty period, spares and technical support remain available.

9. Customs, duties and taxes

Unless the order is DDP, the customer is the importer of record and is responsible for duties, taxes, clearance fees and any registration required in the destination country. We provide the documentation needed to clear the shipment but we do not act as importer of record on the customer’s behalf.
