Ordering, payment and logistics

Indicative list prices cover standard configurations, Ex Works China. Final pricing depends on configuration, quantity, destination and Incoterms, and is confirmed in a written quotation valid for 30 days.

For equipment: 30% deposit with order, 70% against pre-shipment inspection, by T/T or irrevocable L/C at sight. For supply orders under USD 10,000, payment in full with order. Card and PayPal payment is available for samples and small instrument orders.

We quote FOB, CIF and DDP. On DDP we handle freight, duties and clearance documentation to your door. For DDP we will need your full delivery address and tax registration details before confirming.
